🔒 Privacy Policy – Jin Tools Chrome Extension

Effective Date: July 17, 2025

At Jin Tools, we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use the Jin Tools Chrome Extension. By using the extension, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We do NOT collect or store any personal data through the extension. However, we may access or store the following non-personal information locally on your browser:

  • Tool access preferences
  • Activation/license key status
  • Session tokens (locally, not sent to any server)

2. What We Don't Collect

We do not collect or track:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone)
  • Location or IP address
  • Financial or payment details
  • Browsing history or web activity
  • Messages or communications
  • Login credentials or passwords

3. Use of Permissions

Jin Tools uses Chrome permissions solely to:

  • Enable quick access to premium tools
  • Store user activation status
  • Open selected tool pages in a new tab
  • Display extension notifications if needed

We do not misuse or abuse permissions for any other purpose.

4. Third-Party Access

We do not share or sell any user data to third-party services. The extension only opens official websites (like Freepik, Canva, etc.) with no interference.

5. Data Security

The extension does not transmit any data externally. All storage happens locally in the Chrome browser.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
